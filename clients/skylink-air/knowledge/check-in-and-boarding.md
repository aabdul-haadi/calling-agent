# Check-in and boarding

## Online and airport check-in

Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 90 minutes before departure. Airport check-in desks close 60 minutes before departure for international flights.

## Boarding

Boarding starts 45 minutes before departure and the gate closes 20 minutes before departure. Passengers who arrive after the gate closes may lose their seat.

## Documents

International passengers need a valid passport. Check destination visa requirements before you travel.
