# Checked baggage allowance

## How many bags can I check
Economy passengers may check 1 bag free of charge. Flex passengers may check 2 bags. Business passengers may check 3 bags.

## Weight and size limits
Each checked bag may weigh up to 23 kg (50 lb) in Economy and Flex, and up to 32 kg (70 lb) in Business. The maximum size is 158 cm (62 in) length plus width plus height.

## Extra and overweight bags
An additional checked bag costs 60 dollars. Bags between 23 kg and 32 kg in Economy and Flex cost 45 dollars extra. Bags over 32 kg cannot be accepted.
