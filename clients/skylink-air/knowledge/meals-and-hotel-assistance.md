# Meals and hotel assistance during disruption

## Meal vouchers
If your flight is cancelled or delayed more than 3 hours, you receive a meal voucher of 15 dollars at the airport.

## Hotel and transport
If a cancellation means you must stay overnight, SkyLink Air provides a hotel room and airport transfers free of charge when the disruption is within our control.

## Keeping receipts
If you arrange your own hotel or meals because we could not, keep all receipts and submit them within 30 days for reimbursement up to the limits above.
