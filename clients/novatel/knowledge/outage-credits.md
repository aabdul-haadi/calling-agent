# Outages and credits

## Checking for outages
You can check the network status for any coverage area. If your area has an outage, our engineers are already working on it and you do not need to report it.

## Credits for long outages
If your home service is down for more than 24 hours because of an outage on our network, you receive an automatic credit of 2 dollars per full day. Credits appear on your next bill.

## Slow data during maintenance
Planned maintenance can cause slower mobile data for a few hours. No credit is given for planned maintenance under 12 hours.
