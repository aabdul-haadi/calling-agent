# Visual asset licences

## public/visuals/3d: 3dicons (CC0)

Rendered 3D icons from **3dicons** by Vijay Verma (https://3dicons.co, source https://github.com/realvjy/3dicons).

- Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication). Commercial use is allowed, no attribution is required, and the files may be modified and redistributed. Confirmed on the project's GitHub licence metadata (`CC0-1.0`) and on the "What License is 3dicons?" answer at https://3dicons.co/.
- Variant used: the free **Color**, **Dynamic** angle renders only. The site's separately sold "Premium" variants are not used.
- Processing: downloaded as 400 px PNG, trimmed to the icon's bounds and re-encoded as WebP at 96 px and 256 px (`<name>-96.webp`, `<name>-256.webp`).
- Source pattern: `https://3dicons.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/v1/dynamic/color/<name>-dynamic-color.png`.

Alternatives considered and not used: Icons8 (free tier needs attribution and does not allow redistribution of the files), Streamline (free tier needs attribution; the 3D and Color families are paid), Lordicon (animated icons are licensed per plan).
