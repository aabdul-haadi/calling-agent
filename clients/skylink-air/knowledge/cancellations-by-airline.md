# Flight cancelled by SkyLink Air

## What happens when we cancel your flight

If SkyLink Air cancels your flight, you are never charged for the disruption. You can choose a free rebooking on the next available SkyLink flight on the same route, or a full refund to your original payment method.

## How we tell you

We email and text the contact details on your booking as soon as a cancellation is confirmed. You can also check your booking status in the app or ask our virtual agent.

## Time limits

Free rebooking onto an alternative flight is available for 14 days after the original departure date. Refund requests for cancelled flights can be made for up to 90 days.
