# Billing cycle and due dates

## When you are billed

NovaTel bills monthly on the 1st for the previous calendar month. Each bill includes your plan charge plus any extras such as roaming, data packs and one-off fees.

## Payment due date

Payment is due on the 15th of the month after the billing period. You can pay in the NovaTel app, by card, or by direct debit.

## Why a bill can change

A bill is higher than usual when it contains extra charges such as international roaming or an extra data pack. The bill lists every charge as a separate line item.
