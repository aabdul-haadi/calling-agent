# Carry-on baggage

## Cabin bag allowance
Every passenger may bring one carry-on bag and one personal item such as a handbag or laptop bag. The carry-on bag may weigh up to 8 kg (18 lb) and measure up to 55 x 40 x 23 cm.

## Liquids
Liquids in carry-on bags must be in containers of 100 ml or less, all fitting in one transparent resealable bag.

## Items not allowed in the cabin
Sharp objects, sporting bats and any flammable items must be placed in checked baggage or left at home.
