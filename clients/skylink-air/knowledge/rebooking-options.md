# Rebooking options

## Rebooking after a cancellation

When your flight is cancelled by SkyLink Air, we rebook you on the next flight with free seats on the same route at no cost. Your fare class stays the same. If no seats are left in your fare class we will place you in the nearest available class without extra charge.

## Seats and availability

Alternative flights are shown with the number of seats left. Seats are held for you only once the rebooking is confirmed, so please confirm your chosen flight promptly.

## Only one change per conversation

For safety, the virtual agent can make one flight change per conversation. For anything further, a human agent will help.
