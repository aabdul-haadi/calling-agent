# Fare classes

## Economy, Flex and Business
Economy is our lowest fare with one checked bag and one free change. Flex adds a second bag, a lower change fee and refundability up to 24 hours before departure. Business includes three bags, lounge access, no change fees and full refundability.

## Seat selection
Seat selection is free for Flex and Business. In Economy a seat costs 12 dollars, or is assigned free at check-in.
