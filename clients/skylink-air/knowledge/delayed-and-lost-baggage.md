# Delayed and lost baggage

## Reporting a problem
If your bag does not arrive, report it at the SkyLink baggage desk before leaving the airport, or within 7 days online. Claims about lost or damaged baggage are handled by our human baggage team.

## Delayed bags
We deliver delayed bags to your address free of charge. If your bag is delayed more than 24 hours you can claim up to 100 dollars per day for essential items, with receipts, up to 500 dollars.

## Lost bags
A bag is treated as lost after 21 days. Compensation is limited to the declared value, up to 1500 dollars.
