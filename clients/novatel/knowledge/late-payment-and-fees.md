# Late payment and fees

## Late payment fee
If a bill is not paid by the due date, a late fee of 5 dollars is added after 7 days. The fee is charged once per bill.

## Overdue accounts
After 30 days overdue, outgoing calls and data may be limited until the balance is paid. Service is restored within one hour of payment.

## Payment problems
If you cannot pay on time, a human agent can discuss options. The virtual agent cannot change payment dates or agree payment plans.
