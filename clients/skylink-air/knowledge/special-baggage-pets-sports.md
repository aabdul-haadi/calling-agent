# Special baggage, pets and sports equipment

## Pets
Small dogs and cats up to 8 kg including the carrier can travel in the cabin for a fee of 100 dollars each way. Only one pet per passenger, and pets must stay in the carrier under the seat. Larger animals travel as cargo; contact a human agent.

## Sports equipment
Bicycles, skis and golf bags count as one checked bag and are accepted up to 32 kg. Surfboards longer than 210 cm cannot be accepted.

## Musical instruments
Small instruments can travel in the cabin as your carry-on. Larger instruments need an extra seat.
