# Roaming charges

## International roaming

Using your phone abroad costs 6 dollars per day on all plans, up to a maximum of 18 dollars for any 3 days. Daily charges apply only on days you use data, calls or texts.

## Avoiding roaming charges

Switch on airplane mode or turn off mobile data and data roaming in your phone settings before you travel.

## Where roaming charges appear

Roaming appears as its own line on your next bill, for example "International roaming (3 days)".
