# Refunds

## When you can get a refund
You are entitled to a full refund if SkyLink Air cancels your flight and you decide not to travel, or if the new flight we offer does not suit you. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Refund timing
Refunds are processed within 7 business days. Card payments may take an additional 3 to 5 business days to appear on your statement.

## Refunds for voluntary cancellations
If you cancel a flight yourself, refund depends on the fare. Flex and Business fares are fully refundable up to 24 hours before departure. Economy fares are not refundable but you receive a travel credit minus a 50 dollar processing fee.
