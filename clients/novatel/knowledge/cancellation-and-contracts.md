# Cancellation and contracts

## Cancelling your plan
Cancellation requests are handled by a human agent who confirms your identity and the end date. The virtual agent cannot cancel plans.

## Notice period
A 30 day notice period applies to all plans. You pay for service until the end of the notice period.

## Early termination
Plans on a 24 month contract carry an early termination fee equal to 50 percent of the remaining monthly charges.
